Ask about a unit

Good neighbors make a good park

A few clear expectations keep Secluded Pines peaceful.

These highlights are here to help you plan. Park management may update policies when needed, and guests who disregard them may be asked to leave without a refund.

10 mph

Park speed limit—strictly enforced.

Quiet hours and respectful conduct

Quiet hours are 10:00 p.m.–7:00 a.m. Please respect neighbors, keep vehicle idling brief, and keep children at their campsite after 9:00 p.m. unless accompanied by an adult.

Pets

Well-behaved pets are welcome. Keep pets leashed and under control outside your RV, clean up immediately, and never leave a pet tied outside while you are gone. Current rabies vaccination is required.

Parking, sites, and outdoor items

Park and drive on hard surfaces only. Two vehicles are allowed per site. Keep your site clean and uncluttered; no parking, tents, rugs, or mats on grass. Decks require management approval.

Fires, grilling, and trash

Use designated fire pits with approval, and follow burn bans. Do not place grills on picnic tables. Bag trash securely and use designated containers. Do not put glass, metal, plastic, or cigarette butts in fire rings.

Monthly-stay requirements

Monthly stays are application only. RVs must be clean, in good working order, and approved by management. Window air conditioners, exterior water heaters, extra buildings, subleasing, and dog kennels are not allowed. Monthly site fees cover up to four adults; additional-person fees may apply.

Wi-Fi terms

Basic Wi-Fi is provided as a convenience and performance is not guaranteed. Do not use the network for unlawful activity, excessive high-volume transfers, unauthorized access, or hosting services. Use is at your own risk.
