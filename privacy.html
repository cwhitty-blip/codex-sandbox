Privacy

Privacy notice

Effective November 17, 2026

What Pocket Planner stores

Contractors may store company profile information, customer names and contact details, service addresses, job notes, schedules, mileage, estimates, photographs, and other job documents. Account authentication information is handled by our authentication provider.

How information is used

Information is used to provide the contractor workspace, create customer job views, deliver requested job notifications, protect accounts, troubleshoot service problems, and maintain billing and operational records.

Customer job links

A customer job link provides access to the specific job identified in the email. Customers should not forward the link. Links expire, and a contractor can replace an existing link by sending a new one.

Service providers

Pocket Planner uses service providers for hosting, database storage, authentication, file storage, and email delivery. These providers process information only as needed to operate the service.

Retention and deletion

Contractors control their job records. Archived jobs remain available until an authorized owner or administrator permanently deletes them. Some security, billing, and delivery records may be retained when reasonably necessary for fraud prevention, accounting, dispute resolution, or legal obligations.

Your choices

Contact the contractor responsible for a job to correct customer information or request removal. Account owners can export job records and request account assistance through the support page.

Security

We use access controls, expiring customer links, private file storage, encrypted connections, and scoped company records. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so passwords and customer links should be kept private.

Contact

Use the Pocket Planner support page for privacy, access, or deletion requests.
