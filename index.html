Straight answers

Before you start.

Is Pocket Planner only for roofers?

No. It is built for service contractors who need a simple way to organize jobs and communicate with customers.

Does my customer need an account?

No. Customers open their secure job view from the link you send them.

Will it work on my phone?

Yes. Pocket Planner is a web app designed for phones, tablets, and desktop browsers.

What happens after the free trial?

Review the current plan and everything included on our pricing page. Payment details are handled by our billing provider, not stored in Pocket Planner.
