Support

Get the right kind of help.

Contractor account access

Use Contractor sign in and choose “Forgot password?” for a secure reset email. Never send anyone your password or a password-reset link.

Customer job links

Ask the contractor who sent the email to issue a new link when a customer link has expired. For privacy, Pocket Planner support cannot change a contractor’s job record on a customer’s request.

Billing

Paid enrollment is not active during the beta. Pocket Planner will not request payment information until paid checkout is visibly enabled on this domain.

Data correction or deletion

Customers should contact their contractor first. Account owners can archive jobs, export records, and request permanent account assistance by replying to their Pocket Planner beta invitation or onboarding email.

When reporting a problem

Include the page name, what you expected, what happened, the approximate time, and a screenshot with customer details hidden. Never include passwords, customer-link URLs, payment information, or private documents.
