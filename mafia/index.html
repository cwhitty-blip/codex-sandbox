Quick rules

Town: Find and vote out every Mafia player.
Mafia: At night, silently choose one player to eliminate.
Doctor: Choose one player to protect. If that player was targeted, nobody dies.
Detective: Choose one player. The host silently signals whether that player is Mafia.
Day: Discuss, accuse, defend, then vote one player out. Mafia wins when they equal or outnumber the Town.
