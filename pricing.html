Good to know

Pricing questions, answered plainly.

How are job and storage limits measured?

Only current, active jobs count toward the job limit. Completed and archived jobs stay searchable without consuming an active-job slot. Uploaded files count toward the company’s storage allowance.

Will I be charged when I start the trial?

No. A credit card is not required to begin the 14-day trial. Billing terms will be shown before a payment is authorized.

What happens when I reach a limit?

Your existing records remain available. You can archive completed jobs, clean up unneeded files, or upgrade before adding more active work. Pocket Planner will not add surprise usage charges.

Are upcoming features guaranteed?

No. Roadmap items show product direction, not a contractual commitment. We may change the scope or order based on contractor feedback and technical testing.

Will the basics become expensive add-ons?

Our goal is to keep the core job, customer, document, schedule, and communication workflow together rather than create a maze of essential add-ons.
